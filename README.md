## 01 — The Final Chapter.

After nearly seven years of wasting my life writing code, building applications, debugging endless problems, and sacrificing my physical and mental well-being, I have finally walked away from software development.

Seven calendar years filled with endless lines of code, sleepless nights, headaches, neck pain, back pain, and countless hours sitting behind a screen.

I finally found my life, my career, and my peace of mind in a completely different field, far away from programming.

And I have absolutely no intention of going back.

- No more Android.
- No more backend.
- No more programming.
- No more debugging.
- No more sleepless nights.
- No more sacrificing my health for a screen.

The industry I once knew is no longer the same.

The rise of artificial intelligence, AI-generated code, and the increasing dependence on automated development tools have destroyed much of what once made programming meaningful to me.

What was once a craft built on knowledge, patience, creativity, and genuine problem-solving has become something I no longer recognize or wish to be part of.

**The industry changed. So did I.**
